RSU and bonus income in the Bay Area

A general guide to how equity compensation and bonuses are often reviewed on a mortgage file. This is not a promise that RSU income will increase the amount you can borrow.

What is RSU income?

Restricted stock units (RSUs) are a form of equity compensation. An employer typically grants a number of units that convert into shares according to a vesting schedule. Until units vest, they are usually not the same as cash in hand.

Many Bay Area tech and other professional employees are paid a mix of salary, bonus, and equity. Mortgage lenders may consider some of that income when they underwrite a loan, but they do not all treat it the same way. Documentation, vesting history, and how much of the income is recurring can all matter.

How lenders often look at equity and bonus income

As a general matter, an underwriter is trying to decide whether income is stable enough to repay the new loan. For bonus and equity compensation, that review commonly includes:

  • A history of receipt, often over more than one year, shown on pay statements, a W-2, or a tax return
  • Whether shares have actually vested and been delivered, versus a future unvested grant
  • Taxes withheld, share sales, and whether proceeds were used or held as assets
  • Concentration in one employer’s stock, and what happens if employment ends

None of those bullets is a promise that a particular file will qualify, or that a larger loan amount will be available. Under Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines, vested RSUs are counted as income. Jumbo lender guidelines can differ.

Why this comes up in the Bay Area

Purchase and refinance amounts in San Francisco, San Jose, and nearby counties often exceed the conforming limit and become jumbo loans. See the live 2026 Hawaii and California loan limits and the California jumbo refinance page. A jumbo file can involve closer review of income and assets. That is a process point, not a claim about pricing.

What to gather before a call

If equity compensation is part of your pay, it usually helps to have recent pay statements, equity-plan or vesting summaries, W-2s or tax returns, and a brokerage statement showing vested shares or sales. What a lender will actually require depends on the file and the investor guidelines in effect at the time.

Questions

Can RSU income be used to qualify for a mortgage?

Sometimes. Under Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines, vested RSUs are counted as income. Jumbo lender guidelines can differ, and the income still has to be documented. This page does not say that RSU income will increase the loan amount you can obtain.

Do unvested RSUs count?

Under Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines, income is calculated using vested shares. Unvested grants may never vest, and jumbo lender guidelines can differ.

Does this apply only in California?
