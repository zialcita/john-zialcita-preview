Hawaii jumbo refi FAQ

Do you work on neighbor islands?

Yes. John serves Oahu and the neighbor islands.

Can I refinance a condotel or non-warrantable condo?

Often, through portfolio lenders. Eligibility depends on the project and lender; we review your specific property.

Should I refinance?

Refinancing isn't always the right move—it depends on your goals, timeline, and current loan structure. We analyze your break-even point, closing costs, and long-term savings to ensure refinancing delivers real value. If it doesn't make sense, we'll tell you.
