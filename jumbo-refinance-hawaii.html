What counts as a jumbo loan in Hawaii?

A one-unit mortgage in Hawaii is jumbo when the new loan amount is above the FHFA conforming limit for that county. For mortgages Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac acquire in 2026, that one-unit limit is $1,249,125 in Hawaii County, Honolulu County, and Kauai County, and $1,299,500 in Maui County and Kalawao County. Two- to four-unit limits are higher. See the full 2026 Hawaii and California table (source: the FHFA conforming loan limit files). A jumbo cash-out refinance uses the same county test.

Hawaii jumbo refi FAQ

What is the 2026 conforming loan limit in Honolulu?

For a one-unit property in Honolulu County, the FHFA 2026 conforming limit is $1,249,125. Hawaii County and Kauai County use that same one-unit limit. Maui County and Kalawao County are $1,299,500. A loan above the limit for the county and the number of units is jumbo. See the 1- to 4-unit table.

Do you work on neighbor islands?

Yes. John serves Oahu and the neighbor islands.

Can I refinance a condotel or non-warrantable condo?

Often, through portfolio lenders. Eligibility depends on the project and lender; we review your specific property.

Should I refinance?

Refinancing isn't always the right move—it depends on your goals, timeline, and current loan structure. We analyze your break-even point, closing costs, and long-term savings to ensure refinancing delivers real value. If it doesn't make sense, we'll tell you.
