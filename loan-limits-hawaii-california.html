What are the 2026 conforming and jumbo loan limits in Hawaii and California?

For 2026, a one-unit loan is jumbo above $832,750 in baseline California counties, above $1,249,125 in the highest-cost California counties and in Honolulu, Hawaii County, and Kauai, and above $1,299,500 in Maui and Kalawao. The tables below are the FHFA county limits.

What is the difference between a conforming loan and a jumbo loan?

A conforming loan is at or under the loan limit FHFA sets for that county and unit count. FHFA says loans above that origination balance are jumbo loans, because Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac are limited to buying mortgages under the cap.

These figures are not interest rates. Pricing, if you want it, is on the pricing tool, which is not a commitment to lend.

What are the 2026 baseline and ceiling?

FHFA announced on November 25, 2025 that the baseline one-unit limit for 2026 is $832,750, up $26,250 from 2025. The one-unit ceiling in high-cost areas of the contiguous United States is $1,249,125, which is 150 percent of the baseline. For Alaska, Hawaii, Guam, and the U.S. Virgin Islands, FHFA set the one-unit baseline at $1,249,125 and the one-unit ceiling at $1,873,675.

How do I tell if my refinance is jumbo?

Compare the new loan amount with the limit for the county and the number of units. If the origination balance is above that limit, FHFA treats the loan as jumbo. The home price can be higher than the limit without the loan being jumbo, if the loan amount is not.

Loan limit questions

What is the 2026 conforming loan limit in Honolulu County?

For one unit, $1,249,125. Two-unit is $1,599,375, three-unit is $1,933,200, and four-unit is $2,402,625. A loan above the limit for the unit count is jumbo.

What is the 2026 conforming loan limit in Maui County?

For one unit, $1,299,500. Kalawao County uses the same limits. Two-unit is $1,663,600, three-unit is $2,010,950, and four-unit is $2,499,100.

What is the 2026 conforming loan limit in Los Angeles County?

Los Angeles County is at the 2026 contiguous ceiling: $1,249,125 for one unit, $1,599,375 for two units, $1,933,200 for three units, and $2,402,625 for four units. Orange County uses the same limits.

What is the 2026 conforming loan limit in San Diego County?

For one unit, $1,104,000. Two-unit is $1,413,350, three-unit is $1,708,400, and four-unit is $2,123,100. That is above the $832,750 baseline and below the $1,249,125 ceiling.

What is the 2026 conforming loan limit in Sacramento County?

Sacramento County is at the 2026 baseline: $832,750 for one unit, $1,066,250 for two units, $1,288,800 for three units, and $1,601,750 for four units.

Is the conforming limit based on the home price or the loan amount?

FHFA describes the conforming loan limit as a cap on the origination balance of the mortgage. A mortgage above the limit for that county and unit count is what FHFA calls a jumbo loan. It is not a cap on the sale price of the house.

Did conforming loan limits increase for 2026?

FHFA set the baseline one-unit limit at $832,750 and said that is an increase of $26,250 from 2025. County numbers on this page are the 2026 figures. This page does not republish the 2025 county tables.

Where do these numbers come from?

The November 25, 2025 FHFA news release and FHFA full county file for calendar year 2026. This page was checked against those files on October 30, 2026.
