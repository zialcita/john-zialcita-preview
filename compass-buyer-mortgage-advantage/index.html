Compass buyer mortgage advantage

A review draft about a reported pricing credit for some buyers working with a Compass agent. Public reporting is summarized below; John’s own offer is marked for him to confirm.

What this page is about

Some real-estate brokerages have, from time to time, a pricing or credit arrangement for buyers who finance through a participating mortgage lender or broker. Compass is a national real-estate brokerage. This draft describes only what is publicly reported about a wholesale-lender arrangement, and it leaves John’s own participation to him to confirm.

Trade press has reported that Rocket Pro, the wholesale lending arm of Rocket Mortgage, announced a partnership involving Compass buyer’s agents and a broker credit on eligible purchase loans. HousingWire covered that announcement. Those reports are about a lender program, not a personal offer from John, and program terms can be changed or ended.

[JOHN TO CONFIRM] That John currently originates through Rocket Pro (sometimes called Rocket Wholesale) and can offer a Compass-buyer credit to his clients.

[JOHN TO CONFIRM] The current credit amount, eligible loan types (for example purchase only versus refinance), stacking with any other credits, geographic coverage in the Bay Area and Hawaii, and the dates the terms are in effect.

[JOHN TO CONFIRM] Any dollar-savings figure. The previous site mentioned savings such as “up to $10,000” and a “40 basis point” advantage; do not publish those as current fact until John confirms them and the dates.

[JOHN TO CONFIRM] Who is eligible: a buyer represented by a Compass agent or a Compass-affiliated brokerage, and any other conditions.

What this page does not claim

  • It does not say a Compass buyer will receive a lower rate or a specific dollar credit from John.
  • It does not say every loan type, including jumbo or non-QM, is included.
  • It is not an endorsement of a real-estate agent, and it is not a commitment to lend.

If John confirms the program, a buyer should still compare the actual Loan Estimate with other available options. A credit or pricing adjustment is only one line on a loan.

Questions

Is there a public source for a Compass–Rocket Pro credit?

Yes. HousingWire has reported on a Rocket Pro partnership with Compass that included a broker credit on eligible purchase loans involving a Compass buyer’s agent. Read the current article and any lender updates; this site is not the official program description.

Does John offer that credit today?

[JOHN TO CONFIRM] Yes or no, and under which wholesale channel. Until that is confirmed, treat the answer as unconfirmed.

Public source used as background (not a current offer letter): HousingWire, “Rocket Pro Compass partnership adds 40 bps broker credit”. Program terms can change. Confirm with John and with the lender’s current overlay before relying on any credit amount or end date.
