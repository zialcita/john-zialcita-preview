What makes a broker different from a big online lender?

Retail banks are limited to their own product suite and pricing. Brokers compare multiple wholesale lenders simultaneously, matching your profile to the lender with the best combination of rate, fees, and underwriting flexibility.

How long does a jumbo refinance take?

Many refinances close in 21–30 days. Complex scenarios involving multiple properties, self-employment income, or asset depletion may take 30–45 days.

Should I refinance?

Refinancing isn't always the right move—it depends on your goals, timeline, and current loan structure. We analyze your break-even point, closing costs, and long-term savings to ensure refinancing delivers real value. If it doesn't make sense, we'll tell you.
